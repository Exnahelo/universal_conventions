# Proposed semester closeout

Status: policy proposal only; no deletion implementation or automatic schedule.

The intended result is to retain useful generated study material without keeping
unneeded bulky imports forever. A generated summary is not necessarily a complete
replacement for its source: diagrams, annotations, attachments, and citation page
numbers can be lost.

1. The user explicitly identifies a completed semester for review.
2. Inventory its original imports, sizes, derivative links, and provenance. Show
   which sources have no complete usable derivative or cannot be retrieved again.
3. Verify retained material, citations, images, and local links. Retain sources
   needed for ongoing work or required retention; do not assume a summary suffices.
4. Present an exact proposed removal list and expected space saving. Offer an
   external archive where continued access matters.
5. Obtain approval for that concrete list before removing originals. A general
   preference to save space is not authorization to delete an unspecified set.
6. Preserve provenance and a closeout record, including what was removed and how
   to retrieve it if possible. Check retained links after closeout.

Deleting files tracked in Git does not remove historical copies or necessarily
shrink the repository. Any history rewrite requires a separate review of shared
history and recovery needs. Do not promise space recovery from a normal commit.
